## How to read a row

- `mechanical`: a hook exists in NeoForge's event and attribute surface; the
  proposal names the mechanism, not the balance numbers.
- `rework`: no direct hook. The note names the closest implementable
  substitute; a rework still has to be authored, not merely enabled.
- `drop`: cut. Under §6.8 a curse that cannot be enforced mechanically either
  becomes a rework or leaves the set.
- `decided`: the D2 answer replaced the keyword class and note for this row.
- `confidence` reflects keyword evidence only: `high` means two or more
  independent terms in the same class, `low` means a fallback.
