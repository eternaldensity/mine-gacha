## Scoring

Necessity is structural: 3 per candidate that requires this one + 2 if a fit rule names it, capped at 20. Cross-pack adoption is reported beside it as a separate signal rather than folded into necessity.

Suitability = 30 build + up to 10 release type + up to 10 maintenance recency + up to 8 licence + up to 5 server side + up to 8 cross-pack adoption + up to 6 dependents + 4 fit host, capped at 100.

Licence classes: permissive (MIT/Apache/BSD/MPL) 8; copyleft (GPL) 6; non-commercial 4; other 3; all-rights-reserved or custom-protective 2; unknown 0. This is a distribution-risk signal, not legal advice.

Role is structural: `core` is content a fit rule names or that at least two candidates require; `library` and `support` use Modrinth's category metadata; `add-on`, `compatibility` and `client-only` follow the survey section and side metadata; `unusable` has a dependency with no build; `design-conflict` contradicts a settled plan decision; `excluded` has no build.

Verdict is the pack recommendation. Seeds (the original content sections) are included when suitable because they were chosen deliberately; harvested content needs cross-pack evidence; support and add-ons need both adoption and suitability; libraries are marked unused when nothing requires them.
